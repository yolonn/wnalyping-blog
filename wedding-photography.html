常见问题

Everything you need to know about our wedding photography services

How far in advance should we book you for our wedding?

We recommend booking 9-12 months in advance, especially for popular wedding seasons (spring and fall). However, we sometimes have last-minute availability, so it's always worth checking!

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely! We love destination weddings. Travel expenses are calculated separately based on location. We've photographed weddings across the country and internationally.

How many photos will we receive?

For a typical 8-hour wedding, you'll receive 500-700 edited images. We focus on quality over quantity, ensuring each photo is beautiful and meaningful.

What is your photography style?

Our style is a blend of photojournalistic storytelling and classic portraiture. We capture candid moments as they unfold while also creating beautiful, timeless portraits.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. A 30% deposit secures your date, with the remaining balance due 30 days before your wedding.
