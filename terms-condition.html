4. Cancellations & Rescheduling

We understand that circumstances may change. Our cancellation and rescheduling policies are designed to be fair to both parties.

Cancellation Policy:
  • More than 30 days before shoot: Full deposit refund minus a $100 administration fee
  • 15-30 days before shoot: 50% of deposit refunded
  • Less than 15 days before shoot: No refund of deposit
  • Day of shoot/no-show: Full payment forfeited
Rescheduling Policy:
  • More than 30 days before shoot: Free rescheduling to available date
  • 15-30 days before shoot: $100 rescheduling fee applies
  • Less than 15 days before shoot: Treated as cancellation (see above)
Weather & Force Majeure:

For outdoor shoots, we reserve the right to reschedule due to inclement weather. In cases of force majeure (natural disasters, pandemic restrictions, etc.), deposits will be held for rescheduled dates.
