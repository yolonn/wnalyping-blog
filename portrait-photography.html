Portrait Photography FAQs

Common questions about portrait sessions

What should I wear for my portrait session?

We recommend solid colors, avoiding busy patterns. Bring 2-3 outfit options and consider layers for variety. We'll provide detailed wardrobe guidance during your consultation.

How long does it take to receive my photos?

Editing typically takes 2-3 weeks. You'll receive a proof gallery to select your favorite images, which we then professionally edit and deliver in high-resolution format.

Can I choose the location for my portrait session?

Absolutely! We offer studio sessions, outdoor locations, or your preferred setting. We'll help you choose based on your desired style and lighting conditions.

Do you provide hair and makeup services?

Yes, we work with professional hair and makeup artists who can be booked for your session. This service is included in our Classic and Premium packages.

What if I'm uncomfortable in front of the camera?

Most people feel this way! We specialize in making clients feel relaxed and confident. We provide gentle direction and create a fun, comfortable atmosphere throughout the session.
