Our Story

Capturing life's moments through the lens since 2012

Meet Sarah Mitchell

Founder & Lead Photographer

With over 12 years of professional photography experience, Sarah's passion for capturing authentic moments began during her studies at the New York Institute of Photography. What started as a love for street photography evolved into a thriving studio that now serves clients across the globe.

Sarah believes that every photograph should tell a story - whether it's the joyful tears of a bride, the proud smile of a CEO, or the quiet beauty of a product. Her approach combines technical precision with artistic intuition, creating images that are both beautiful and meaningful.

"Photography isn't just about taking pictures. It's about preserving memories, telling stories, and creating art that lasts a lifetime."

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped LuxeLens Studio

2012

The Beginning

Sarah Mitchell graduates from NYIP and opens a small studio in Brooklyn, focusing on portrait and wedding photography.

2014

First Major Recognition

Wins "Best Wedding Photography" award at New York Photography Awards, leading to increased visibility and client base.

2016

Studio Expansion

Moves to current Manhattan location and hires first assistant. Adds commercial and product photography services.

2018

International Recognition

Work featured in Vogue and Harper's Bazaar. Begins shooting destination weddings and international campaigns.

2020

Digital Transformation

Expands virtual services, develops online client portals, and invests in state-of-the-art digital equipment.

2024

Today & Beyond

Full-service studio with 8 team members, serving clients worldwide across all photography disciplines.
