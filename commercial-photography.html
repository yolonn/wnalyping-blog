商业摄影 常见问题

Everything you need to know about our commercial photography services

What's included in a commercial photography quote?

Our quotes include creative direction, photography time, basic editing, and specified usage rights. Additional costs may include location fees, talent, specialized equipment, and advanced retouching. We provide detailed, itemized quotes for complete transparency.

How do you ensure our commercial photos align with our brand?

We start with a comprehensive brand discovery session, reviewing your brand guidelines, target audience, and marketing objectives. We then develop a creative brief that aligns photography style, lighting, and composition with your brand identity and business goals.

Can you work with our marketing agency or internal team?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with marketing agencies, creative directors, and in-house marketing teams. We can serve as an extension of your team or work seamlessly with your existing agency partners to ensure cohesive execution.

What is your typical turnaround time for commercial projects?

Small projects: 1-2 weeks. Medium campaigns: 3-4 weeks. Large campaigns: 4-6 weeks. We offer rush services for urgent projects and provide detailed timelines during the planning phase. Most clients receive initial proofs within 48-72 hours after the shoot.

Do you offer video production alongside photography?

Yes, we offer full video production services including commercial videos, social media content, product demos, and corporate videos. Many clients choose to combine photography and video shoots for maximum efficiency and consistent branding.
