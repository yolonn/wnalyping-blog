Fashion Photography FAQs

Everything you need to know about our fashion photography services

What is included in a fashion photography package?

Our fashion packages include creative direction, photography, basic retouching, and delivery of high-resolution images. Additional services like advanced retouching, styling, and hair/makeup can be added as needed.

Do you provide models and styling for fashion shoots?

Yes, we have partnerships with modeling agencies and professional stylists. We can handle full production including model casting, wardrobe selection, hair, and makeup based on your requirements and budget.

How long does post-production take for fashion images?

Basic retouching takes 5-7 business days. Advanced retouching or large volumes may take 10-14 days. We provide timelines based on the specific requirements of your project.

Can you shoot at our preferred location or studio?

Absolutely. We can shoot at your preferred location, our studio, or scout locations that match your creative vision. Location fees and travel expenses are calculated separately.

What formats do you deliver the final images in?

We deliver high-resolution JPEG files suitable for print and web. We can also provide TIFF files for print production and optimized web versions. Files are delivered via secure online gallery.
