热门推荐 Questions

Quick answers to our most frequently asked questions

Booking

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3-4 months in advance for weddings and 2-3 weeks for portrait sessions. During peak season (May-October), wedding bookings fill up 6-12 months in advance.

Pricing

What's included in your packages?

全部 packages include professional photography, edited high-resolution images, print release, and online gallery. 婚礼 packages also include a second 位摄影师, engagement session, and custom wedding album.

Preparation

How should we prepare for our session?

We provide a detailed preparation guide for each type of session. Generally, we recommend comfortable clothing, good rest, and arriving relaxed. For portraits, bring 2-3 outfit options.

Booking & Process

How do I book a session with LuxeLens Studio?

Booking is easy! You can start by:

  1. 联系我们ing us through our website form or calling us at (555) 123-4567
  2. We'll schedule a free consultation (in-person, phone, or video call)
  3. Discuss your vision, needs, and answer all your questions
  4. Select your package and sign the contract
  5. Pay your deposit to secure your date

We'll then send you a detailed preparation guide and schedule your session.

What is your cancellation/rescheduling policy?

We understand that plans change. Our policy is:

  • Rescheduling: Free rescheduling up to 14 days before your session (30 days for weddings)
  • Cancellation: Deposit is non-refundable but can be applied to future sessions within 12 months
  • Weather: Outdoor sessions can be rescheduled due to inclement weather at no charge
  • Illness: We offer free rescheduling if you or a family member is ill

Full details are in our contract which you'll receive before booking.

Do you travel for photoshoots?

Yes! We love traveling for photoshoots. Our travel policy:

  • Local: Included within 30 miles of New York City
  • Regional: Travel fees apply beyond 30 miles ($0.75/mile)
  • Destination: We offer destination photography worldwide. Travel expenses (flights, accommodation) are additional
  • International: Available with proper notice and travel arrangements

Preparation

What should we wear for our photoshoot?

Wardrobe can make a big difference! Our recommendations:

  • Coordinate, don't match: Choose complementary colors rather than identical outfits
  • Avoid logos and busy patterns: Solid colors photograph best
  • Consider the location: Dress appropriately for the setting
  • Bring options: We suggest 2-3 outfit changes for variety
  • Accessorize: Jewelry, hats, and layers add visual interest
  • Comfort is key: Choose clothes you feel confident and comfortable in

We provide a detailed wardrobe guide specific to your session type after booking.

How long does a typical photoshoot last?

Session durations vary by type:

  • 人像 Sessions: 1-2 hours
  • Family Sessions: 1-1.5 hours
  • Engagement Sessions: 1.5-2 hours
  • 婚礼 Coverage: 6-10 hours (depending on package)
  • Commercial Shoots: 2-8 hours (project dependent)
  • Drone Sessions: 1-3 hours (location dependent)

We never rush sessions and allow extra time for outfit changes, breaks, and perfecting shots.

Can we bring pets to our session?

Absolutely! We love pet-friendly sessions. Here are some tips:

  • Check the location: Ensure pets are allowed at your chosen location
  • Bring a handler: Have someone who can watch your pet when not in photos
  • Favorite toys/treats: Bring their favorite items to keep them engaged
  • Consider timing: Schedule when your pet is typically calm and happy
  • Clean-up supplies: Be prepared for any accidents

We've photographed everything from dogs and cats to horses and exotic pets!

How long until we receive our photos?

Our standard delivery times:

We retain copyright to all images, but you receive:

航拍服务s

Are you licensed to fly drones commercially?

Yes! We are fully certified and compliant:

  • FAA Part 107 Certified: 全部 pilots hold current FAA remote pilot certificates
  • Insured: $1 million liability insurance coverage
  • Regulations: Fully compliant with all FAA regulations and airspace restrictions
  • Safety First: Strict pre-flight checklists and safety protocols
  • Equipment: Professional-grade drones with redundant safety systems

We secure all necessary permits and authorizations for restricted airspace when required.

What are the weather limitations for drone flights?

We prioritize safety and quality. Flight limitations include:

  • Wind: Maximum sustained wind of 20 mph
  • Precipitation: No flying in rain, snow, or fog
  • Temperature: Optimal range: 14°F to 104°F (-10°C to 40°C)
  • Visibility: Minimum 3 miles visibility required
  • Lightning: No flying within 10 miles of thunderstorms

We monitor weather closely and will reschedule if conditions aren't ideal. Drone sessions include a weather contingency plan in the contract.

Can you fly drones at our wedding venue?

In most cases, yes! Here's our process:

  1. We check FAA airspace restrictions for your venue
  2. 联系我们 the venue to confirm drone policies
  3. Obtain necessary permissions/waivers if required
  4. Plan safe flight paths that won't disrupt your event
  5. Coordinate timing with your wedding schedule

Some venues have restrictions, especially indoor facilities or certain historical sites. We handle all the research and paperwork as part of our drone service package.
